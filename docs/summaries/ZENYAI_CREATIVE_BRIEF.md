# ZENYAI CREATIVE BRIEF
## Master Reference for All Video Generation

---

## 🎯 COMPANY MISSION & CORE MESSAGE

**Zenyai exists to solve the broken bridge between audio and visual workflows.**

### The Problem We Solve:
- Sound designers waste hours tagging, organizing, and syncing audio files
- Creative teams lose momentum searching for the right sounds
- Audio workflows are fragmented, manual, and inefficient
- Professionals spend more time managing than creating

### Our Solution:
- **AI-native audio asset management** with intelligent auto-tagging
- **Video-to-audio synchronization** that understands context and emotion
- **Cloud collaboration** that keeps teams in sync
- **Semantic search** - find sounds by describing intent, not filenames

### Core Value Proposition:
**"Make sound creation feel like discovery, not labor. Make creativity frictionless."**

## 🎨 BRAND IDENTITY & VISUAL STYLE

### Primary Visual Style: **Claymation/Stop-Motion**
- Rich textures where you can see fingerprints in clay
- Slightly jerky motion like real stop-motion
- Handcrafted, imaginative, and surreal feel
- Bridges traditional craftsmanship with AI precision
- Symbolizes: "Human creativity meets artificial intelligence"

### Color Palette:
- **Primary:** Deep Violet/Purple (#7D3FEF) - intelligence, imagination
- **Secondary:** Electric Cyan (#00E0FF) - technology, precision
- **Accent:** Warm Coral/Pink (#FF6B6B) - creative energy
- **Neutrals:** Soft Cream (#F5F2F0), Charcoal Gray (#222222)

### Lighting Style:
- Cinematic but playful
- Glowing purples, pinks, and orange highlights
- Warm golden tones transitioning to electric purples
- Volumetric fog for mood, specular highlights for tangibility

## 🎬 VIDEO STRUCTURE & PACING

### 8-Second Format (4 beats x 2 seconds):
1. **Beat 1 (0-2s):** Establish chaos/problem
2. **Beat 2 (2-4s):** Escalation/character introduction
3. **Beat 3 (4-6s):** Solution demonstration/transformation
4. **Beat 4 (6-8s):** Brand reveal + spoken "Link in bio"

### Camera Language:
- Dynamic movement with slight handheld jitter
- Wide → Medium → Close-up progression
- Shallow depth of field for cinematic feel
- Focus pulls for dramatic emphasis
- Parallax motion for realism

## 📝 PROMPT CREATION GUIDELINES

### Always Include:
- **Format:** "9:16 vertical, 8 seconds, four 2-second beats"
- **Style:** Specific visual aesthetic (Claymation, photoreal, etc.)
- **Character motivation:** Why they're speaking to camera
- **Emotional stakes:** What's at risk, what's gained
- **Brand integration:** Natural, not forced
- **Spoken CTA:** "Link in bio" delivered by character

### Technical Requirements:
- **Resolution:** 720x1280 vertical
- **Duration:** 8 seconds maximum
- **Audio:** Synced, cinematic sound design
- **Motion:** Smooth but urgent transitions
- **Text:** Minimal, readable, motion-synced
- **Brand colors:** Purple dominant with accents

### Narrative Structure:
- **Hook** (0-2s): Grab attention with visual/situation
- **Build** (2-4s): Escalate tension/stakes
- **Reveal** (4-6s): Show solution/transformation
- **Close** (6-8s): Brand + CTA with personality

## ⚡ CREATIVE PRINCIPLES

### 1. Transformation Over Information
- Show change, don't just explain features
- Chaos → Order visual metaphors
- Before/After emotional states

### 2. Personality Over Polish
- Imperfect, human touches
- Character quirks and humor
- Authentic reactions and emotions

### 3. Context Over Features
- Show real use cases, not abstract benefits
- Emotional stakes, not technical specs
- Human problems, AI solutions

### 4. Flow Over Friction
- Smooth, urgent pacing
- No dead time or filler
- Every second serves the story
